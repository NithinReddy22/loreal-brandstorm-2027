---

## 1. Standardized Evidence Categories

All claims, numerical metrics, and technical features across the OptiChroma project are classified into eight mutually exclusive evidence categories:

1. **Implemented and unit-tested:** Software algorithms written in Python with passing automated test cases verifying mathematical operations and edge-case handling. *(CRITICAL GOVERNANCE NOTE: Unit tests verify software execution only; they never prove optical physics or clinical utility.)*
2. **Demonstrated on synthetic images:** Algorithms evaluated on synthetic checkerboards, blurred fields, or synthetic color targets in software.
3. **Demonstrated on physical optical hardware:** Empirically measured using physical benchtop instruments (integrating sphere, spectrometer, oscilloscope, power supply, phantoms).
4. **Demonstrated in a usability study:** Evaluated through human-factors testing or user interviews with patients/pharmacists under an approved non-interventional protocol.
5. **Supported by published literature:** Traceable to peer-reviewed photomedicine, dermatology, or computer vision publications with primary citations.
6. **Proposed engineering target:** A design specification or performance goal derived from theoretical principles, awaiting physical empirical verification.
7. **Commercial assumption:** Business model, pricing, or production cost hypothesis requiring formal market and vendor validation.
8. **Future clinical validation requirement:** Prospective, IRB/ethics-approved clinical trials with patient populations required for diagnostic agreement or therapeutic claims.

## 3. Mandatory Presentation Governance Rules

1. **State the True Identity:** *"OptiChroma is an accessible optical smartphone accessory and standardized visual documentation platform designed to improve image consistency for remote dermatological communication."*
2. **Never Present Software Tests as Optical Proof:** The 42 passing unit tests verify that the Python code executes as written on synthetic arrays; they do NOT prove physical optical performance on human skin.
3. **Always Label Proposed Targets:** Never present the $\ge 75\%$ glare reduction target or the $s_w \le 0.40$ repeatability target as verified results until benchtop measurements are logged in `OPTICHROMA_v2_4_VALIDATION_PLAN.md`.
4. **Transparent Hardware Status:** Acknowledge that while the TPS61165 series-boost driver circuit has been calculated and simulated, benchtop physical assembly and measurement are required before hardware sign-off.
